## About the Game (long description)

**Every shift is the same. Walk the route. Check the list. Sign it. Again.**

SUBSTRATE is an atmospheric horror game about the bureaucracy of noticing.
Your handheld terminal lists five fixtures. You inspect each one and log it:
PASS or FLAG. If your log matches reality, the shift closes and the next
round begins. If it doesn't, you walk the route again.

**The facility does not match the checklist.**

Something is growing down here. It moves only when you aren't looking. Lamps
you logged yesterday aren't on their mounts. A corridor is longer than you
remember. And your terminal — your only tool — has no field for any of it.
You can see it. You cannot report it. The shift will not close until every
listed item is signed off.

**Your memory is the game.**

- No monsters chase you. No death screens. The horror is that the *building*
  is wrong, and only you know it.
- Failure never resets — it exposes. An unclosed shift means walking the
  route again, and the growth has advanced while you walked.
- Learn the route until you know it cold. That knowledge is the only thing
  that lets you notice — and noticing is the only thing that matters.

**2–3 hours. One facility. It gets worse.**

## Capsule & screenshots

- The lighting pass already produces store-quality frames — the teal sewer
  with red practicals reads instantly. Capture at 3840×2160, no UI.
- Capsule concept: the handheld terminal in the foreground (checklist visible,
  one row flagged), a dark corridor behind it, one green lamp glowing. The
  checklist IS the hook — put it on the capsule.
- Six screenshots: 2× atmosphere (corridor, chamber), 2× terminal-in-context
  (logging a lamp; the SHIFT NOT CLOSED message), 1× growth mid-ladder,
  1× an *absence* (empty mount, [ ] row visible). The absence shot is the
  whole game in one image.
- Trailer line (30s cut): normal round → same shot next round, lamp missing →
  terminal shows no field for it → title. No jump scare. End on the checklist.
