  - Cause Explanation:
    The corridor floor slabs (StaticMeshActor_2741, etc.) sit at Z = 280..288 cm,
    spanning X: [-1800 .. -1200]. StaticMeshActor_3691 was intended as an invisible
    safety barrier over the central canal water (X > 0). However, because its X scale
    was set to 41.7, its collision volume extended across X = 0 into negative X space
    all the way to X = -1500.
    The collision box floated from Z = 290 cm to Z = 330 cm (10 cm above the corridor floor).
    Because the player capsule base is at Z = 288 cm and the box top was at Z = 330 cm,
    the vertical step was 42 cm. Since character MaxStepHeight is 45 cm, any player capsule
    sweep directly struck the front face of this invisible box at Y ~ 8150..8379,
    completely halting forward movement while the visual floor ahead was entirely clear.
