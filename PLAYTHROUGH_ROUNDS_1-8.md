# Playthrough: rounds 1–8 at normal pace

**Build:** `Packaged\Windows_DevPlaythrough_20261024_205855\Substrate_ShowPosition.bat`

The top-left overlay line `Location: V(X=…, Y=…, Z=…)` is your position. When something is wrong, take a screenshot that shows it (Win+Shift+S) and write one line about it.

**Controls:**
- **F** raises or stows the terminal.
- **E** logs PASS.
- **Q** logs FLAG.
- **L** turns the flashlight on or off.
- Close the shift at the trigger next to spawn.

## What to note

Write down only what stands out. A blank round is useful information too.

| Round | What the design says happens | What I want to know |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Nothing. You learn the route and the checklist. | Did the route and the terminal make sense with no help? Did it drag? |
| 2 | Exactly one fixture disappears (Absence). | Did you notice it on your own? How long did it take, and where were you? |
| 3–7 | 1–2 anomalies per round: missing fixtures, dead lights, growth, the extra variants. | Which ones you spotted, which you only noticed late, and any you think you missed. Was anything too dark to judge? |
| any | A shift won't close. | Was it clear why? Did walking the route again feel tense or just annoying? |
| 8 | The shift closes into Sector D and the ending. | Did it feel earned after the full run? |

## Always report

- Any fall, stuck spot, invisible wall or teleport (screenshot plus position).
- Any moment you were bored, and the rough round and time it happened.
- Anything you saw that wasn't on the checklist, even if you're not sure it was intended.
