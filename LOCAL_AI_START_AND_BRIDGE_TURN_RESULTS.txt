    the player capsule penetrated RouteEnd_ShiftTrigger, firing OnShiftAuditFailed() and applying
    a red camera fade before shift tasks were started.
  - Root Cause 2 (Dock Foundation Fall):
    Dock foundation concrete slabs StaticMeshActor_792..796 had CollisionEnabled = NoCollision.
    Stepping off decorative detail cubes dropped the player through the foundation to Z=104.
  - Status: Repaired in previous iteration (RouteEnd_ShiftTrigger relocated to canal return stairs
    at (300, 1100, 300); StaticMeshActor_792..796 set to BlockAll).

FAILURE B (POST-BRIDGE RIGHT TURN): User crossed bridge, turned right, then fell into water.
  - The Facing Direction & Coordinate Math:
    The player crosses the bridge Westbound (moving in the -X direction).
    When facing West, character yaw is 180.0 degrees:
      - Forward is -X (West)
      - Backward is +X (East)
      - RIGHT is South (-Y, towards lower Y coordinates)
      - LEFT is North (+Y, towards higher Y coordinates)
    The previous repair incorrectly assumed "turning right" meant moving North (+Y) toward
    Fixture C-05. Consequently, the previous repair only fixed collision on northern slabs
    (3106..3115) and placed fences only along the northern canal edge (Y: 8420..9070).
  - The Physical Floor Void (Missing Collision on South Corridor):
    The bridge landing stairs deposit the player at X ~ -2450, Y ~ 8240, Z ~ 310.
    Directly south of Y = 8188 lies the South West Bank Corridor (Route R09 / R08):

      - StaticMeshActor_2716: SM_concrete_01 slab spanning X: [-2595..-2396], Y: [7608..7894], Z: 288.0

      - StaticMeshActor_2717..2720, 2876..2877, 3206..3207: continuing all the way south to Fixture C-03.
    EVERY SINGLE ONE of these southern walkway floor slabs had:
      BodyInstance.CollisionProfileName = 'NoCollision'
      BodyInstance.CollisionEnabled = 'NoCollision'
  - The Landing Debris Steering Funnel:
    Visual debris piles (StaticMeshActor_2960, 2965, 3006, etc.) sit at X: [-2520..-2430], Y: [8140..8260].
    When a player descends the bridge stairs and naturally steers right (South, -Y) around the debris

  - The Fall Transition:
