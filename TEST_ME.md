# Test list — SUBSTRATE

Launch the packaged build (no editor needed):

    Substrate\Packaged\Windows\Substrate.exe

Windows may show a firewall prompt on first launch. The game is offline
single-player — dismissing or denying it is fine.

## The one question

**Walk Sector D and tell me whether the ending is worth keeping.**

You reach it by closing the shift on round 8. The facility grants you access to
a sector it has spent the whole demo insisting does not exist, drops you at the
mouth of a corridor, and talks to you three times as you walk. At the far end is
**C-06 RELIEF VALVE** — the phantom checklist row that has been unreportable
since round 5. Press E or Q on it. Either works. That press ends the demo.

What I need from you is not whether it runs. It's whether arriving at C-06 lands
as a payoff, or as an anticlimax after a long walk.

## What changed

Sector D was built on top of existing sewer geometry — including a full-height
wall inside a locked group that could not be moved. The whole sector has been
relocated to empty space and verified three ways before you see it: full-length
traces across the walk lane at three widths and three heights, downward traces
for continuous floor, and a body stepped through six stations along the corridor
confirming each one holds.

The "lights die behind you" effect is gone. It was fighting the corridor's job of
being walkable — those three lights were the only lighting in the sector — and it
cost three of your test runs. The three spoken beats remain.

## Known gaps

- **Sector D is silent.** Ambience needs your ear; my four solo attempts at audio
  all failed, so I left it alone rather than guess again.
- The demo-end fallback timer is 15 minutes, so it will not cut a run short.
- Steam: still waiting on Valve. Nothing to do until that email arrives.
