SUBSTRATE - demo 0.9
====================

You are the maintenance technician for a sealed facility. Each shift you walk the same route, check the same
fixtures and log them on your handheld terminal. Between shifts, things change. Your job is to notice.

START
  Run Substrate.exe. The game opens on your terminal, in the dome:
    E   start (or continue) your shift
    Q   new game from shift 001
    C   Overtime - unlocked once you finish the demo

CONTROLS
  WASD / mouse   move / look            F     raise or stow the terminal
  E              log PASS (looks right) Q     log FLAG (something is wrong or missing)
  C              photograph a fixture   L     flashlight
  Esc            pause menu: settings (look sensitivity, volume, brightness, invert Y), restart, quit

A SHIFT
  1. In the dome, log the three dome fixtures (comms desk, generator, ration shelf).
  2. Walk into the green-lit door: the airlock to the sewer (Sector C).
  3. Log C-01 to C-05 along the canal. The amber "TRANSIT" airlock beside the sewer arrival opens when they are done.
  4. Log B-01 to B-03 in the transit corridor, then take the airlock back.
  5. Take the green "SECTOR A - DOME" airlock home and walk up to the tall console marked CLOSE SHIFT.

  Every row on the terminal is a physical object you can find. If something looks wrong - or is simply gone -
  log it with Q. A missing fixture is still logged: stand where it was, look at the spot, press Q.
  Not sure? Press C to photograph it: a photographed fixture counts as reported (3 photos per shift).

  Walking into the console with a row logged wrong fails the audit (red flash). The shift stays open: fix the row
  and try again.

WHAT WE WANT TO HEAR
  - Where you got lost, stuck or confused (a screenshot and one line is perfect).
  - Anomalies you spotted, ones you only noticed late, and ones you think you missed.
  - Whether the ending landed, and whether you would play Overtime.

Saves are kept automatically between sessions (Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Substrate\Saved\SaveGames).
